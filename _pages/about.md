Welcome! This page was last updated on 15/10/2026.

My name is Jingxuan (Jason) Xu. I got my BSc in Mathematics through a joint program between [Xi'an Jiaotong Liverpool University](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/study/undergraduate/applied-mathematics) (2019-2021) and [University of Liverpool](http://liverpool.ac.uk/mathematical-sciences/) (2021-2023). I later earned an MPH in Biostatistics from [Yale University](https://ysph.yale.edu/public-health-research-and-practice/department-research/biostatistics/), with my thesis advised by Professor [Shuangge Ma](https://ysph.yale.edu/profile/shuangge-ma/), Professor [Zhenqiu Lin](https://medicine.yale.edu/profile/zhenqiu-lin/) from [Yale Center for Outcome Research and Evaluation (CORE)](https://medicine.yale.edu/core/), and Professor [Hao Mei](http://stat.ruc.edu.cn/jxtd/jsdw/swtjylxbxx/f19457567e31409488f1396d3538a2a0.htm) from Renmin University of China. Now I am a PhD student in Statistics at [Michigan State University](https://stt.natsci.msu.edu/), advised by Professor [Hao Zhang](https://directory.natsci.msu.edu/directory/Profiles/Person/104733).

I am braodly interested in probability, statistics and machine learning. My current research interest lies in the field of optimal transport, with application in spatial statistics. My current project focuses on developing a framework for Wasserstein likelihood-free inference and model checking methodology for spatial models, or more generally, statistical models on random fields.
